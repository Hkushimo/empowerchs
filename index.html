What Empower Does

Support for the details that can make care hard to access.

Community Health Workers meet people where they are. Empower helps clients understand available options, organize information, communicate needs, and connect with the right local supports.

How Support Works

A simple path from referral to follow-up.

Empower starts with the referral, learns what the person needs, and helps coordinate the next step. The focus is steady communication, practical support, and connection to services that fit the situation.

  • Review the referral and contact information
  • Understand immediate needs and barriers
  • Coordinate resources, appointments, and referrals
  • Follow up so clients are not left on their own

Additional Needs

Practical needs can be included in the referral.

If housing-related support, recovery stability, or clean and safe living concerns are part of the situation, include that in the form. Empower will review the request and follow up with the right next step.
