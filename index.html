What Empower Does

One team for the needs that often overlap.

Health, housing, recovery, and clean living spaces are connected. Empower helps clients understand their options, stay organized, and get connected with services that support safer and more independent living.

Recovery Housing

Safe, structured, supportive living.

Empower Recovery Housing offers a path to stability, recovery, and independence for people who need a structured sober living environment.

  • Fully furnished shared rooms
  • Utilities included, including heat, electric, and Wi-Fi
  • Weekly house meetings and peer support
  • Daily structure, accountability, and recovery stability support
